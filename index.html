🔒 Your health data never leaves your device

Track Blood runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account, no upload. Every PDF you drop stays on your machine — the app reads it locally, extracts the numbers, and hands you a CSV. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, ever.

Want to verify? Turn off your Wi-Fi and try it — it still works perfectly.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Track Blood free?

Yes. Track Blood is completely free on iOS, Android, and the web, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does Track Blood upload my blood reports to a server?

No. Track Blood is local-first — every PDF you upload is parsed and stored only on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server or cloud account.

Which labs and report formats does it support?

Track Blood recognizes over 150 markers and their aliases across formats from labs including Thyrocare, SRL, Metropolis, Apollo, and Aarthi Scans, regardless of how each lab names or lays out its tests.

Is there an iOS and Android app, or just the website?

All three — this website works fully in the browser, and native iOS and Android apps are available with the same local-first parsing, plus journaling and reminders.

Can I export my data to share with a doctor?

Yes — Track Blood generates a single CSV or PDF with every parameter across every report date, so a doctor can see trends at a glance instead of flipping through individual reports.
